All three parts of our Yellowstone documentary in one film, about 35 minutes. Part 1 goes underground: what is really beneath the supervolcano, why "overdue" is a myth, and why steam explosions are the real danger. Part 2 is the 30 years when the U.S. Cavalry ran the world's first national park: poachers, a stagecoach robbery and the birth of the rangers. Part 3 follows the Nez Perce, as many as 800 people and nearly 2,000 horses, across Yellowstone in the summer of 1877, running from the Army.

Filmed on the ground by us in October 2021, with public-domain archival photographs and maps, and clearly labeled AI-assisted illustrations for moments no camera recorded.
