[   0.08-   6.08] Neil Ericsson was a Swedish immigrant who had fought in the Geronimo campaigns as a cavalry sergeant,
[   6.08-  11.08] and who would later become the very first ranger of the Chiricahua National Forest.
[  11.08-  16.08] Emma Peterson, also from Sweden, had worked as a maid for an army colonel.
[  16.08-  23.08] They met at Fort Bowie, married in 1887, and claimed a homestead in Benita Canyon.
[  23.08-  29.08] Neil later wrote that Emma had found the place and thought it was the most gorgeous spot she had ever seen.
