On July 23, 2024, the ground at Yellowstone's Biscuit Basin exploded without warning. Steam, mud and rock shot about 600 feet into the air, and none of the park's seismometers, GPS stations or satellites saw it coming. So what is actually under Yellowstone? Not a countdown clock, and not a supervolcano that's "overdue." The real story, from the USGS Yellowstone Volcano Observatory and two 2025 studies in Nature, is stranger.

In this episode: the three super-eruptions that built the park; the hotspot that started in Nevada; the magma "sponge" that's only 9-15% melted and the gas-rich lid in "steady breathing"; why the USGS says Yellowstone is NOT overdue (the odds are about 1 in 730,000 a year); the steam blasts that are the real danger; the 1959 earthquake that woke 160 new geysers; the hot-spring microbe behind PCR; and the last 23 wild bison.

Filmed on the ground by us in October 2021: Grand Prismatic Spring, Norris and Steamboat Geyser, Mud Volcano, West Thumb, the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone, Mammoth and Mesa Falls.
