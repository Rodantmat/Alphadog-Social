[   0.02-   5.02] The Chiricahua Apache called this place the land of the standing up rocks.
[   5.02-   9.02] The settlers who came later called it the wonderland of rocks.
[   9.02-  12.02] Even the name of the mountains is old.
[  12.02-  17.02] Chiricahua is usually traced to an Apada word for wild turkey.
