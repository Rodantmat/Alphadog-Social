AlphaDog Studio Publisher

Connect your TikTok account

AlphaDog Studio Publisher posts AlphaDog Studio's own documentary clips to its own TikTok account on a schedule.

When you connect, TikTok asks you to allow:

  1. Basic profile (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): show which account is connected.
  2. Account stats (user.info.stats): followers, likes and video count on our dashboard.
  3. Your videos (video.list): confirm each scheduled post went live.
  4. Post and upload (video.publish, video.upload): publish our clips, or send them to TikTok drafts.

Continue with TikTok

You can disconnect at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
