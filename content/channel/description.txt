Strange histories of American places.

AlphaDog Studio makes long-form documentaries about the places on the American map that hold more story than they let on: new national parks, ghost towns, lost frontier forts, sacred mountains, outlaw country and forgotten landmarks.

Each episode digs into the real history, the legends people still tell, and the land itself, built from archival photographs, historic film, maps and 3D terrain flyovers of the actual ground.

New episodes regularly. Subscribe and tell us in the comments which place we should explore next.

Narration is AI-generated. Sources and image credits are listed in each video's description.
