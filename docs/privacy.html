AlphaDog Picks · Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

AlphaDog Picks Publisher ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the AlphaDog Picks accounts on Instagram and TikTok. It is used only to publish videos and images that we create to our own accounts, and to read engagement on our own posts.

What data the app accesses

When the account owner authorizes the app, it receives access tokens and basic profile information (account ID, username, profile picture) for that account, plus the posts, comments and insights of that account. It does not access any other user's private data.

How data is used

Tokens are used only to publish our own content, reply to comments on our own posts, and read our own post performance. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in our private automation environment and are never published. Public comments may be processed to write replies and are not stored beyond what is needed to reply.

Deletion

To revoke access, remove the app in your Instagram/Facebook or TikTok settings. To request deletion of any data related to you, contact us at the email below and we will delete it within 30 days.
