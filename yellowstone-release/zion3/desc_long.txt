In 1927 two crews began digging toward each other from opposite ends of a mile of solid sandstone in Zion. This is the story of how a canyon nobody believed in became a national park, and how the road to it was cut through the cliffs: Dellenbaugh's paintings at the 1904 World's Fair, the park by petition, Presidents Taft and Wilson, the railroad and Harding's visit, the dead end, and the Zion-Mount Carmel Tunnel, dedicated July 4, 1930.
