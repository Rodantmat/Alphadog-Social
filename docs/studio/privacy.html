Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

AlphaDog Studio Publisher ("the app") is operated by AlphaDog Studio to publish its own content to its own social media accounts.

What we access

When we connect our own accounts, the platforms (TikTok, Meta, Google/YouTube) give the app an access token and basic account information (account ID, username, display name, avatar) plus statistics about our own posts. We use this only to upload our videos, write captions, reply to comments on our posts and measure how our posts perform.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share any data. We do not collect personal data from viewers or other users, we do not track people, and we do not use the data for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published. Post records (date, platform, post ID, link) are kept as a publishing log.

Deleting data

We can revoke the app's access at any time in each platform's settings, which invalidates the tokens. To ask about or delete any data, send us a message on Instagram (@alphadog.studio) or Facebook (AlphaDog Studio).
