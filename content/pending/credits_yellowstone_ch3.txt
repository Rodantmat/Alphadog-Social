ILLUSTRATED SCENES (AI-ASSISTED)
• Illustrated animation made for this video with open-weight models (Qwen-Image and Wan 2.2, Apache-2.0): riders in the Lower Geyser Basin, the Big Hole, the captured tourists and George Cowan's ordeal, the release of the captives, the river crossing, the feint and escape past Sturgis, and the siege at Bear Paw. These are illustrations, not real footage.
