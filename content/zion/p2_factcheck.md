## Fixes made during the check (script now reads as below)
- **s019:** "carried sand and mud down from the plateau" became "down the canyon". No source says where the fill came from.
- **s025:** "it built a rail line to Cedar City" became "A rail spur reached Cedar City, Utah, in 1923". NPS says the spur was completed; it does not say who built it.
- **s026:** "much of the wood came down on a wire" became "lumber, brought down from the plateau on a wire". NPS says all 265,000 board feet came that way.
- **s046:** cut "the greenest part of the canyon" (unsourced).
- **s054:** cut "a canyon most Americans had never heard of" (unsourced).
- **Cut entirely:**
  - the Central Park 275-ft comparison (verified, but cut for length);
  - a derived claim that the Angels Landing trailhead would have been underwater (our arithmetic, not stated by a source);
  - the 1992 Springdale earthquake landslide (verified by quake.utah.edu, but cut for length);
  - the Feb 2025 Scenic Drive rockfall (verified by the brief only, cut for length);
  - "full access returned in late May" (Wikipedia only).
