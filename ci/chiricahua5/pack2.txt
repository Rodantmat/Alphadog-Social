On October 20, 2026, Chiricahua became America's 64th national park — and almost nobody knows its story. An ancient volcano built it. Apache warriors defended it. Outlaws hid in it. A gunfighter died under an oak tree here in 1882, and the case was never really closed.

In this documentary: Geronimo's surrender and the Apache scout Massai, who jumped from a prisoner train and made his way home; Cochise and the Bascom Affair; the eruption that built the rocks; the jaguar caught on camera; the blind "Lady Boss" of Faraway Ranch; and who really killed Johnny Ringo.

A note on respect: this episode does not identify or speculate about burial or ceremonial sites. The new park law protects traditional cultural and religious sites and guarantees access for tribal members.
